04 / the 3D contract

The model is a diagram.

Orthographic, two inks, no glow. The 3D stage is the same drawing the print would use; the labels sit on the perimeter with leader lines.

Camera

Orthographic, 32° feel, no perspective distortion. Drag rotates the model; scroll zooms; double-click resets.

Ink

Black geometry, red route. No shading, no bloom, no gradient. The red is the one live signal.

Labels

Numbered leaders on the perimeter. Four labels max. The label is part of the drawing, not a floating tooltip.

Fallback

The print is the fallback. A static SVG with the same numbers, same caption, same scale.

05 / the rules

Clarity is the style.

If a decoration does not help a buyer read the account, it is removed.

Do

  • Two inks plus paper; red is the risk signal.
  • One display face, one reading face, one mono.
  • Visible grid and real figure captions.
  • Numbers in mono; units always labelled.
  • Let the diagram carry the brand.

Don't

  • No shadows, glow, glass or gradient panels.
  • No rounded cards or pill controls.
  • No more than two inks on a plate.
  • No paragraph in Anton.
  • No floating labels over the 3D model.
