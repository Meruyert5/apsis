The model is a wireframe.

One geometry, no shading, acid lines on black. The 3D stage is the same data as the TUI, just projected. The labels are brackets.

camera

42° perspective, 13 units. Drag, scroll, double-click reset. The wireframe is the model, not an illustration.

ink

Acid line, bone node, black field. No glow, no gradient, no shading. The red is the cut.

labels

[bracketed] perimeter labels. Four max. The ASCII TUI is the 2D fallback, not a separate product.

fallback

Use 2D / reduced motion. The static frame is the same wireframe, drawn once.

Content is the chrome.

No decoration that a terminal could not render. That is the whole bet.

do

  • Monospace everywhere; one family.
  • Two neutrals plus one accent.
  • 1px borders; no radius; no shadow.
  • Real commands and real output.
  • Keep the TUI and the 3D in sync.

don't

  • No glass, glow or gradient panels.
  • No pill controls or large radii.
  • No invented terminal output.
  • No colour without a label.
  • No ASCII that is not the data.
