The scene is the instrument.

One shared geometry, four material languages. Drag with damping, scroll to zoom, double-click to reset. Pause offscreen; keep a 2D fallback.

Camera

40° perspective, 11.8 units. One scene per deck. Drag with damping; scroll 0.7–1.7×; double-click resets.

Material

Additive cyan and blue, amber star, 0.22 line opacity. Glow only on the route and the primary, never on a card.

HUD

Four perimeter labels max. Scanlines at 5% and corner ticks. Labels sit on the frame, not over the geometry.

Fallback

2D SVG + static frame. The Use 2D control is a real control; reduced motion holds the scene without the loop.

What keeps it from becoming sci-fi.

The console is allowed to be a console. It is not allowed to be a toy.

Do

  • One act ink; the gradient is the act, not the wallpaper.
  • Glass on the hero and one readout, not on every card.
  • Glow only for live state: the route, the primary, the LED.
  • Drag, zoom and a 2D fallback on every 3D stage.
  • 14px floor, 44px controls, real focus rings.

Don't

  • No five-accent rainbow on one screen.
  • No pulsing every panel for attention.
  • No floating labels over the orrery.
  • No fake coordinates or invented telemetry.
  • No motion that hides the data.
